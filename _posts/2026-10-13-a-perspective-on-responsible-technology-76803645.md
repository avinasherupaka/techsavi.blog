Your AI agent passed the demo. Would it pass an audit? 🎯

Most teams still treat governance as a gate at the end of the build. In regulated environments, that is backwards.

- ✈️ Think of the flight recorder.
It does not stop a single failure. It makes every flight investigable, so the whole system can learn, improve, and scale with trust.

AI agents need the same property.

- 🛡️ A three-part pattern worth testing:

• Record: log inputs, tool calls, retrieved context, and outputs so any decision can be reconstructed.
• Constrain: define what the agent may access and do, enforced by architecture, not by prompt wording.
• Review: route high-impact outputs to a named human owner with a clear escalation path.

- ⚙️ Why this matters to the business:

• Faster validation, because evidence is generated as a by-product of operation.
• Clearer accountability when something goes wrong.
• Less rework before a system can go into real use.
